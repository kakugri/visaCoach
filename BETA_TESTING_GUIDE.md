# VisaCoach Beta Testing Guide

Use this when sharing VisaCoach with early testers.

## Link

```text
https://visacoach.kakugri.dev
```

## What To Try

Ask testers to complete one short practice session:

1. Open the homepage.
2. Choose a destination and visa type.
3. Add a little context:
   - home country or current residence
   - school, employer, host, or purpose
   - funding source
   - return plan or home ties
4. Start the interview simulation.
5. Answer all five questions.
6. Copy the summary.
7. Optional: create an account and confirm the session appears in saved history.

## Safety Note For Testers

VisaCoach is for practice only. Do not paste passport numbers, bank statements, private immigration records, or other sensitive documents. The app is not legal advice and does not guarantee any visa outcome.

## Feedback Questions

Ask these after the test:

1. Did you understand what to do on the first screen?
2. Did the questions feel realistic for your visa path?
3. Did the feedback feel useful, too generic, too harsh, or too long?
4. Did you know what to improve after reading the final summary?
5. Did the saved profile/account flow make sense?
6. Was anything confusing, broken, slow, or visually awkward?
7. Would you use this again before a real interview?
8. Would you rather see deeper visa coaching or a new mode such as job interviews?

## What To Record Internally

For each tester, record:

- visa path tested
- whether they completed a session
- whether they copied the summary
- whether they created an account
- top confusion point
- top requested improvement
- whether they asked for a non-visa mode

Use [BETA_FEEDBACK_LOG.md](./BETA_FEEDBACK_LOG.md) as the shared internal template.
